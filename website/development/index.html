Ginga Koro · 4.10 Games

Development timeline

A short record of what has been added to the game and its companion pages, from the first playable build to the current work in progress.

  1. 14 October 2026

    Built the playable foundation: ship movement, enemies, shooting, lives, score, pickups, timed stages, difficulty and the first title screen.

  2. 15 October 2026

    Added the first boss, named stages, selectable Astra, Kestrel and Comet ships, bombs, energy pickups and distinct stage backgrounds.

  3. 16 October 2026

    Completed the original five-stage run and added pause controls, animated combat effects, shared formations and the development Lab.

  4. 17 October 2026

    Brought formation profiles into regular play and added development cheats for quicker iteration.

  5. 18 October 2026

    Created the layered title presentation and entrance animation, then separated and refined the home and settings screens.

  6. 19 October 2026

    Introduced the three production ships, banking and propulsion animation, the first Gallery, shield pickups and richer scrolling scenery.

  7. 20 October 2026

    Established signature enemy patterns and hazards, refined pickups and expanded the game’s combat palette.

  8. 21 October 2026

    Expanded the Gallery with animated hazard previews and completed the first major palette and effects refinements.

  9. 22 October 2026

    Added speed upgrades, early sound effects, clearer bomb feedback and animated Gallery effects.

  10. 23 October 2026

    Expanded the game through stages six to ten, added Cave-inspired boss patterns and broadened the Gallery.

  11. 24 October 2026

    Built out NEON OVERDRIVE and began IRON MONSOON, with new stage art, enemy rosters and formations.

  12. 25 October 2026

    Added more campaigns, including the complete CONSOLE WARS stage set, and established campaign-specific identities.

  13. 26 October 2026

    Reworked the front end around Play, Options and Gallery, added campaign selection and full-screen campaign artwork.

  14. 27 October 2026

    Completed the four Special weapons, charged attacks, additional ship concepts and substantial scenery updates.

  15. 28 October 2026

    Added themed playable ships, a broader sound set, music controls and visualiser, and began VEILED CONTACT.

  16. 29 October 2026

    Completed VEILED CONTACT, expanded campaign soundtracks, refined Gallery navigation and added STRANGE HORIZONS backgrounds.

  17. 30 October 2026

    Completed IRON MONSOON with its soundtrack, five stages and a substantial pass on its industrial artwork.

  18. 31 October 2026

    Introduced DANMAKU as a playable mode and improved its ship-fired bullet patterns and performance foundations.

  19. 1 November 2026

    Created the Ginga Prism interface font, added captain identities and presets to ship selection, and upgraded the Ships Gallery.

  20. 2 November 2026

    Expanded high-resolution Gallery artwork, restored the original astronaut, refined the home menu and added persistent homing missiles.

  21. 3 November 2026

    Added saved high scores with player initials, campaign and ship, plus a celebratory High Score Gallery screen and campaign-clear presentation.

  22. 4 November 2026

    Added the 4.10 Games startup splash, campaign victory flow, home music, new boss and enemy artwork, and a clearer pause menu.

  23. 5 November 2026

    Added the illustrated in-game How to Play guide, difficulty-aware scoring records, improved spawn protection and the PLAYGROUND stage framework.

  24. 6 November 2026

    Created detailed 256×512 DANMAKU and IRON MONSOON backgrounds and established the Gallery’s shared logo-background treatment.

  25. 7 November 2026

    Expanded boss backgrounds in the Gallery.

  26. 8 November 2026

    Upgraded CLOCKWORK EMPIRE and NEON OVERDRIVE stage art and boss arenas.

  27. 9 November 2026

    Completed the five CONSOLE WARS stage backgrounds, reorganized the Gallery and built the multi-wave Invaders Must Die! mode.

  28. 10 November 2026

    Finished Invaders Must Die! and its Milky Way backdrop, created the promotional website and campaign pages, and captured gameplay across the game.

  29. 11 November 2026

    Refined CONSOLE WARS stage conversions and boss arenas, and improved native ships, hazards and bosses against the shared Gallery backdrop.

  30. 12 November 2026

    Renamed the game Ginga Koro, completed STRANGE HORIZONS hazard polish, refreshed the encyclopedia and added native boss arenas and campaign artwork to the site.

  31. 13 November 2026

    Added a gameplay video and public development timeline, refined ASHEN FOUNDRY and introduced bespoke weapons for VOLT and CLASSIFIED. Updated the homepage ship and weapon showcases, and added repeatable movement routes for DANMAKU elites and THOUSAND FOLD.

  32. 14 November 2026

    Added TYPE X as a third playable PLAYGROUND mode: a six-wave side-scrolling prototype with Astra, formation enemies, upgrade drops and a pause between waves.

  33. 15 November 2026

    Built Type X’s custom ship, scrolling space backdrop and forward fire, then added bespoke enemy waves with weaving movement and staggered shots. Each wave now runs in three sets, with consistent bullet colours and short gaps.

  34. 16 November 2026

    Completed Type X’s six-wave run and added DMG (ISH), a five-minute Neon Frontier tribute in four greens. Refreshed gameplay captures, the Type X Gallery, homepage ship roster and credits.

  35. 17 November 2026

    Extended Type X to 100 waves with five new enemy designs, destruction effects and a two-level homing-missile upgrade. Added AFTERGLOW, PETAL IGNITION across Playground and BOSS ASSAULT for campaign bosses.

  36. 18 November 2026

    Integrated approved boss movement routes into campaign play and added Captains and Campaigns sections to the Gallery.

  37. 19 November 2026

    Aligned Type X enemy sprites with their references and added outward weapon upgrades. Completed the latest text-casing pass and added playable soundtrack previews to campaign pages.

  38. 20 November 2026

    Completed the boss-movement rollout with Veiled Contact and Iron Monsoon, tuned weapon drops by difficulty and brought the approved bomb effect into gameplay. Split production and development ROM builds.

  39. 21 November 2026

    Standardized section headings, spacing and background fades across the promotional site, added a Packaging Concepts headline and unified the 4.10 Games project footer.

  40. 22 November 2026

    Refreshed the selector, High Score Gallery and Laser Grid from current-ROM mGBA captures; captured all six How to Play screens, removed obsolete HUD and Stage backgrounds images, and kept copy naturally wrapped.
